CRO notes

A few things worth knowing about conversion

Plain notes for founders, checked against published checkout research. Nothing here needs a tool or an agency.

Note 01

Most carts are never checked out

Baymard's average of 50 studies is 70.22% of carts left behind (updated September 2025). Much of that is browsing, not a broken checkout.

Note 02

Surprise costs are the top reason people quit

Set browsing aside and extra costs lead. In Baymard's US study, 40% blamed shipping, tax or fees. Show the total early.

Note 03

Forced sign-up costs you orders

In that study, 18% left because the site forced an account. Let the first order check out as a guest.

Note 04

Shoppers buy on fit, not features

"Is this right for me?" matters more than a long spec list. Answer it above the fold, in the shopper's own words.

Note 05

Check every screen, not just yours

Shoppers arrive on phones, tablets and laptops. Review each layout on its own, on a real device, before calling a change done.

Note 06

Fix one thing, then measure

Smaller stores rarely have the traffic for clean A/B tests. Change one thing at a time and compare a few weeks before and after.
