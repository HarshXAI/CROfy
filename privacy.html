Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

What we collect

  • What you type into the form: your product page link, your email or phone number, and anything you write about your store.
  • Where you came from, if the link you clicked carried it (for example utm_source=email), and the time you sent the form.
  • How the site is used. We use Microsoft Clarity and Vercel Web Analytics to see page views, clicks and anonymised session recordings. Clarity may set cookies.

Why

Only to record your free video, send it to you, and follow up about your store if you reply. We don't send newsletters and we don't sell or share your details with anyone.

Where it's kept

Form entries go to a private Google Sheet and an email to our inbox, readable only by the two founders. Analytics data stays with Microsoft Clarity and Vercel under their own policies.

How long

We keep your entry while we're in touch about your store, and delete it when you ask.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@crofy.in. We'll act on it within 7 days.
